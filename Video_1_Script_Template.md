**Team:** Astrophel (Labony Sur & Aupurba Sarker)

**Visual:** High-quality NASA B-roll of Mars surface, dissolving into an extreme Earth desert.

**Labony (Voiceover):** "Hello, we are Team Astrophel. I am the Team Leader, Labony Sur, and my teammate is Aupurba Sarker. We are answering the challenge to 'Identify Earth Locations that Analog the Permanent Moon Base Locations and Mars'."

## Part 2: Create Empathy for the Problem (0:45 - 1:45)
**Visual:** Clean slide with bold text: "The Problem" alongside a video clip of an astronaut struggling or a rover in rough terrain. 
**Aupurba (Voiceover):** "Testing rovers and habitats requires Earth analogs—locations that mimic off-world terrain. Currently, space agencies rely on a handful of well-known sites like the Atacama Desert. But as we plan diverse missions to the Lunar South Pole and Martian craters, these few sites are no longer enough. We need a way to discover undocumented, highly specific geological analogs on Earth to ensure mission safety."

## Part 3: Your Big Idea - The Innovation (1:45 - 2:45)
**Visual:** UI Mockup of "The Parallels" platform showing a split-screen match (Mars on one side, Earth on the other).
**Aupurba (Voiceover):** "Our solution is 'The Parallels'. It is a data-driven matchmaking platform between planets. Instead of manual geographical surveys, our platform algorithmically compares multidimensional data profiles."
**Visual:** System Architecture Diagram. Show a flowchart of datasets (ASTER, MODIS, LOLA, MOLA) combining into an algorithm icon.
**Aupurba (Voiceover):** "We extract Earth topography using the ASTER Global Digital Elevation Model, and climate data using MODIS. We cross-reference this with Lunar topography from the LRO LOLA dataset, and Martian data from MOLA. By clustering elevation variance and surface temperatures, we can discover hidden Earth locations that structurally match proposed space bases with mathematical precision."

## Part 4: Impact & Next Steps (2:45 - 3:50)
**Visual:** Three clean icons appearing on screen: Cost Reduction (dollar sign shrinking), Risk Mitigation (shield), Open Access (globe).
**Labony (Voiceover):** "The impact of The Parallels is significant. By automating the discovery of analog sites, we reduce mission planning time and costs. Furthermore, we democratize space research, allowing smaller space agencies and universities to find local analogs in their own countries rather than traveling globally."
**Visual:** Final Logo Slide: "The Parallels" by Team Astrophel. A URL or GitHub link at the bottom.
**Labony (Voiceover):** "During the hackathon, we will build out the clustering algorithm and the interactive digital twin viewer. Thank you for your time, and we look forward to bringing The Parallels to life."
